---
title: 精准采集插件
id: oritech:machine_silk_touch_addon
type: block
---

精准采集插件可使自动化采矿机器直接采集方块，而非掉落其常规资源。

该升级插件适用于：
- **[方块破坏器](@oritech:destroyer_block)**
- **[末影激光器](@oritech:laser_arm_block)**

<Callout variant="warning">
    If both a Silk Touch Addon and a
    [Yield Addon](@oritech:machine_yield_addon) are installed on the
    same machine, Silk Touch will take priority.
</Callout>
